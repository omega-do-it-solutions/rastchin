## Uses that do not require permission

You may:

- refer truthfully to the RastChin project or an official RastChin release;
- link to this repository, `rastchin.tools`, or an official marketplace page;
- quote or capture the interface for reviews, tutorials, reporting, research,
  compatibility discussion, and other nominative references;
- state accurately that your work is compatible with, built from, or derived
  from RastChin, provided the statement does not imply endorsement;
- redistribute an unmodified official artifact while preserving its license,
  notices, integrity, and clear source information; and
- use the project name in a repository fork description to identify its origin,
  while giving the fork a distinct displayed product name and logo.

Use only an official, unmodified logo for these references. Keep it readable,
do not distort or recolor it, do not place other artwork inside it, and leave
clear space around it.

## Forks and modified distributions

The Apache-2.0 license allows forks and modified distributions. A modified build
must nevertheless be clearly distinguishable from an official RastChin release:

- choose a distinct displayed name and logo;
- describe the relationship as a fork or derivative, not an official release;
- do not reuse official marketplace identifiers, signing identities, or release
  channels; and
- make your modifications and support responsibility clear to users.

You may retain factual copyright, license, attribution, and source-history
references to RastChin. Those notices should not be removed merely to rebrand a
fork.
